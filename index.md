## 📖 Overview
The **cv-resume-builder-mcp** is an AI-powered tool that simplifies creating your resume and CV. It syncs achievements from platforms like Jira, Credly, LinkedIn, and git. Stay updated with a professional CV without the hassle.

## 💡 Features
- **AI-Powered**: Leverage advanced technology to enhance your CV.
- **Automatic Sync**: Automatically update your accomplishments.
- **ATS-Compliant**: Ensure your resume meets application tracking systems.
- **PDF Output**: Generate CVs in high-quality PDF format.
- **User-Friendly**: Designed for users with no technical skills.

## 📦 System Requirements
- **Operating System**: Windows, macOS, or Linux.
- **Python**: Version 3.6 or higher.
- **Storage**: At least 100MB of free space.
- **Memory**: 4GB RAM or more recommended.

## 💬 Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Model Context Protocol?
The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is a framework that allows the application to bring together and manage data from different sources efficiently.

### Can I use this application offline?
Yes, once installed, you can use the application without an internet connection, except for syncing data from online platforms.

### How do I export my CV?
After creating your CV, click on the “Export” button and choose the PDF format. Your CV will be saved to your preferred location.
